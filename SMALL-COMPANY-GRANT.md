# Small Company Grant

In addition to the permissions in the PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0
(LICENSE), the licensor, Ashkan Kamyab, grants every Small Company the same
permissions for any purpose, including commercial purposes, under the same
terms and conditions, including the Notices requirement.

A Small Company is a company or other organization that, counted together
with every organization under common control with it, has fewer than 5
individuals working for it. Count everyone who works for it: owners,
employees and independent contractors, full-time or part-time. Control means
owning more than half of the voting rights, or having the power to direct
the management, of an organization.

If an organization stops being a Small Company, this grant ends for it
32 days later, unless it has obtained a commercial licence from the licensor
by then.

Every other organization needs a commercial licence for any use that the
PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0 does not allow. To ask for one, open
an issue titled "Commercial licence" in
https://github.com/ashkmb/treant-memory or contact the licensor through
https://ashkmb.com.
